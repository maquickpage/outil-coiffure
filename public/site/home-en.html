How much does it really cost?

From €9.90 incl. VAT/month (2-year commitment), €17.90 incl. VAT/month (1-year commitment), or €29 incl. VAT/month with no commitment. Free .fr or .com domain for 1 year. No setup fees. Final prices, no surprises, no hidden options.

Why is your price so low?

Because everything is automated. No intermediary agency, no custom quotes, no €80/h project manager. Our development teams built an infrastructure capable of generating and maintaining thousands of websites simultaneously. You get industrial efficiency at industrial prices.

No. The editor is designed to be used from your phone. Editing text or adding a photo works just like Facebook or Instagram.

Yes. Hosted by Hetzner in Germany (EU), GDPR compliant. No banking data stored with us (handled by Stripe, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified). Privacy policy compliant with the 2022 Omnibus directive. See our privacy policy (in French).
